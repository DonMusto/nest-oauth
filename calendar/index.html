magnets calendar

A private, single-user connector that shows one household’s own Google Calendar on the self-hosted home-automation screens in that home.

This application has exactly one user: its author. It is not distributed, not published to any app store, not offered as a service, and accepts no sign-ups. It exists so that the author’s own calendar appears on a wall screen in the author’s own living room.

What it does

The author runs Home Assistant, open-source home-automation software, on hardware in their home. This application is the OAuth client used by Home Assistant’s built-in Google Calendar integration:

  • it reads upcoming events from the calendars the author’s Google account can see, so they can be shown on a touch screen at home and used in the home’s own automations (for example, a reminder before an appointment);
  • it adds an event to the author’s calendar only when the author explicitly asks Home Assistant to do so.

Where the data goes

Between Google’s Calendar API and a server on a private home network owned and operated by the author, and nowhere else. There is no hosted backend, no analytics, no third-party processor and no other recipient of any kind. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Scope of access

The author’s own Google account grants the access, and can revoke it at any time from Google Account permissions. Revoking access immediately and permanently ends this application’s ability to reach the calendar.
