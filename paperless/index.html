magnets paperless

A private, single-user connector between one person’s own Google Drive and the self-hosted document archive in their home.

This application has exactly one user: its author. It is not distributed, not published to any app store, not offered as a service, and accepts no sign-ups. It exists so that documents saved to one folder in the author’s own Google Drive end up in the author’s own archive at home.

What it does

The author runs Paperless-ngx, an open-source document archive, on hardware in their home. This application is the OAuth client that lets that archive exchange files with one folder of the author’s Google Drive, using the open-source tool rclone:

  • every few minutes, documents the author places in My Drive/Paperless/Inbox (for example, a receipt saved from a phone) are moved into the home archive;
  • once a night, the archive’s original documents are copied back to My Drive/Paperless/Library, so the author has an off-site copy in their own Drive.

Where the data goes

Between the author’s own Google Drive and a server on a private home network owned and operated by the author, and nowhere else. There is no hosted backend, no analytics, no third-party processor and no other recipient of any kind. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Scope of access

The author’s own Google account grants the access, and can revoke it at any time from Google Account permissions. Revoking access immediately and permanently ends this application’s ability to reach the Drive.
